# Questions to answer in YOUR PRODUCT-ASSUMPTIONS.md

The assignment requires you to write PRODUCT-ASSUMPTIONS.md yourselves, so I have not written it. These prompts only help you think. Delete this file when you are done.

1. What exactly are you building, in one or two sentences? Who is the first person you picture using it?
2. Which real problem do busy people with a chronic condition (or at risk of one) face today? Where have you seen it yourself?
3. Which startup or product inspired you (Product Hunt, Antler India or Y Combinator)? What will you do differently?
4. Who are your target users? Age, job, daily routine, comfort with apps.
5. What is the single main use case on a normal day?
6. Which 3 to 5 features are core?
7. What are you assuming about users? For example: they will log data, they own a smartphone, they want short nudges.
8. What is in your MVP?
9. What is NOT in your MVP (real AI, medical advice, wearables, backend)?

Tip: write in your own words, short and honest. Wrong assumptions are fine; unstated ones are the problem.
